Who is he ?

Lawrence Lek is a London-based Malaysian Chinese artist who unites video games, filmmaking, and electronic music in a singular cinematic universe. He is best known for his immersive installations that explore spiritual and existential themes through the lens of science fiction. Lek is the winner of the 2024 Frieze Artist award and was named by Time as one of the 100 most influential people working in AI.

Artistic Background and Education

He studied architecture at Trinity College, Cambridge, the Architectural Association and Cooper Union in New York. He is represented by Sadie Coles HQ, London.

How does he characterize the media in which he works?

“I make site-specific media installations, films, video games, and virtual worlds. Prior to that, I was doing a lot of architectural installations that were driven by this question of what public art might be today. My interest has always been: how do I use all the affordances that my tools give me? That involves a lot of work with music and soundtracks as well.”

Art Style and Themes

Lawrence Lek's work explores contemporary themes such as virtual reality, simulation technology, and artificial intelligence. He often discusses identity, memory, and sociopolitical issues through fictional narratives. His artistic style has futuristic elements and critical reflections on the technological world.

Technology and creative methods

His approach to making art using video game engines, 3D software and soundscapes.
